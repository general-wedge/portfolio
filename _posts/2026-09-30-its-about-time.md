I've been meaning to start writing... well, anything, for a **very** long time. I've always put this off for one reason or another but today, my friends, today we venture into the wide world of journaling. 

Truthfully, this idea started as a way for me to reinforce my learning around software and AI (if it wasn't obvious, yes, I am a software developer) but a technical blog doesn't seem all that interesting, and there are many interests that are worth musing about.

Thinking about what I will write about, I don't think there will be any pattern to these posts, again, I don't want to write a purely technical blog, - I will most *definitely* be writing some technical posts though - so it is likely it will be whatever peaks my curiosity and interest at the time. As I sit here watching the Toronto Maple Leafs (**its our year**) play the New York Islanders, I'm tempted to make a whole post about what I, as a couch coach, think of the line ups, Looking at you Raddysh and Riley...*yikes*

This is a new habit for me, and one that will need to be nurtured if there is to be any sort of cadence to these posts. There won't be any promises here of when and how often these posts will come, actually, I'm pretty proud I've gone this far, let's hope I continue on this momentum, I am motivated to, but talk is cheap, let the cadence speak for itself.

I will end it here, short and sweet - *I also want to go watch the hockey game*. I'm excited to begin this journey and can't wait to see where it takes me. Here is to starting new things and building life long habits. Huzzah!
